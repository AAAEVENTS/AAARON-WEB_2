A system, not a single product.

A membrane is only as good as what sits under it and around it. This is the build-up we plan for on a roof, terrace or podium.

1

Prepare the substrate

Clean, repair and profile the surface. Moisture, cracks and contamination are checked before anything goes on.

2

Prime

A compatible primer bonds the system to the substrate. Primers are not interchangeable between systems.

3

Waterproof

A liquid membrane or a sheet system, chosen for exposure, movement and access. Water stops reaching the slab.

4

Detail the weak points

Joints, upstands, drains and penetrations get reinforcement fabric and extra build, because that is where leaks start.

5

Protect and inspect

A protection layer, curing time and the checks agreed in the scope, then handover records.

Before we begin.

Answers about the enquiry and application process.

What should be included in a quotation request?

Project location, service or material, application area, substrate condition, scope and expected timing. Technical specifications and photos help with early review.

Can I share technical datasheets and RFQ drawings?

Yes. Attach eligible files when the full enquiry server is deployed, or send them directly by WhatsApp/email while using the standalone site.

Will AAARON assist with product selection?

We can discuss relevant systems for a project's needs. Final use must be aligned with the current product TDS, substrate and exposure conditions.

Can AAARON support both materials and application?

AAARON specialises in construction chemical applications and can discuss material systems and execution as required by the project scope.
